Overview

RPC Public Company Limited

RPC Public Company Limited grew out of the energy business. Formerly Rayong Purifier Public Company Limited, it was founded in 1995 with a plant at Map Ta Phut, Rayong, and is a licensed oil trader under Section 7. Today it is a holding company with Pure Thai Energy Company Limited (“PTEC”) as its core energy company, extended into real estate development through SAMCO.

1. Energy business

Fuel retail business

Pure Thai Energy Company Limited (“PTEC”), the core company, operates the fuel retail business. Pure Thai’s service stations began changing over to the “Caltex” trademark in September 2024, and as of the end of December 2025 there were 80 service stations in total, selling the following products

  • High-speed diesel
  • Gasohol
  • Other products

The Company holds PTEC as a subsidiary operating the fuel retail business, with a policy of elevating the brand and the customer experience by converting service stations to Caltex, a world-class brand with high standards. The main supporting factors are as follows:

01 / 04
  1. 01

    Brand strength

    Caltex is an international fuel brand with a network spanning many countries, well known for the quality of its fuels and service.

  2. 02

    High-quality fuel technology

    Caltex fuels come with Techron® technology, which helps clean the engine and improve combustion efficiency.

  3. 03

    Supply chain collaboration

    Partnering with Caltex gives PTEC access to a more efficient network for sourcing and distributing products.

  4. 04

    Business continuity

    The brand change helps PTEC compete over the long term and respond to changing market trends.
