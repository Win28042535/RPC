Operating strategy

From mission
to action

The Company’s strategy is to invest in businesses related to its core business, while seeking new projects or businesses with growth potential that benefit and support the Company’s operations. Each investment is assessed on its potential, risk factors and feasibility, so that the Company can analyze investments appropriately and generate stable returns for shareholders and stakeholders. The Company aims to invest in a sufficient proportion to take part in managing and setting the direction of key businesses, while improving internal efficiency and modernizing its information technology systems to drive business strategy accurately, transparently and sustainably.
