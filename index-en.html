About RPC

Grown from the energy business
into a holding company with energy at its core

Formerly Rayong Purifier Public Company Limited, founded in 1995 by Thai entrepreneurs experienced in petroleum and petrochemicals, with a plant at Map Ta Phut, Rayong, and licensed as an oil trader under Section 7. Today it is a holding company with PTEC as its core energy company.
