Dividend policy

At least 50% of net profit

The Company pays dividends of not less than 50% of net profit after deduction of all reserves, unless there is reasonable necessity otherwise. Dividend payments take into account factors including:

  • Financial position and liquidity
  • Business expansion plans
  • Other factors relevant to the management of the Company

gavelBoard resolutions to pay dividends require shareholders' approval, except interim dividends, which the Board may approve and report at the next shareholders' meeting.

Subsidiary

Sammakorn PCL (SAMCO)

About 60% of net profit from the separate financial statements, after a legal reserve of at least 5% of annual net profit and any accumulated losses, until the reserve reaches 10% of registered capital.

Other subsidiaries

Considered case by case

Based on financial position, cash flow, liquidity, investment plans and other factors at the subsidiary board's discretion, without materially affecting normal operations.
