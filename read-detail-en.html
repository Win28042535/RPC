RPC expands solar rooftop installations at its service stations

RPC Public Company Limited is installing more solar rooftop systems at its Caltex service stations to cut energy costs and greenhouse-gas emissions from operations.

The project is part of the Group's sustainability approach, bringing clean energy into the energy business while maintaining quality service. Electricity generated is used on site for lighting, dispensers and convenience stores.

Lower costs, greater sustainability

Solar power reduces daytime electricity costs, when demand is highest, and keeps station operating costs competitive over the long term.

“Investing in clean energy creates long-term value for shareholders, communities and the environment at the same time.”Management, RPC Public Company Limited

The Group plans to extend installations across its network and will report progress in its sustainability disclosures and Form 56-1 One Report.
