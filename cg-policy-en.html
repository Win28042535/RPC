Corporate governance

Our commitment

The company is committed to conducting its business under the good corporate governance principles of the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and the Stock Exchange of Thailand (SET), together with the recommendations of the Thai Institute of Directors (IOD) in its Corporate Governance Assessment Report. We recognise that good corporate governance, transparency and accountability build the confidence of every stakeholder and support the sustainable development of the business.

The company has therefore issued a corporate governance policy, published on its website, and a written code of business ethics and conduct. Both are communicated to executives and employees at every level as the standard that directors, executives and employees follow in their work.
